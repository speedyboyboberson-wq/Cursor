The house

Not just a lunch counter.

Restaurant, bar, liquor, and grill — rebuilt as one address on Georges Road.

New Brunswick

A Dominican corner with a nightlife spine.

3 Corner Restaurant Bar Liquor & Grill opened as a neighborhood Dominican room: steam-table lunches, chicharrón, plantains, and a staff that regulars still mention by name. The building itself was gutted and reworked — two bars, package-goods refrigeration, takeout and dine-in, plus a downstairs salon for events, with that gold 3 on the far wall.

The food is still the food: pernil, pollo guisado, rabo, mofongo, pescado empanizado, ceviche, tres leches. The difference is the hour. Carmen’s at 68 Georges Road and La Cabañita on Elizabeth Street feed the same cuisine through the afternoon. 3 Corner is the one that still has a DJ, a liquor case, and a last pour after midnight.

On this block

How we sit among the neighborhood

Carmen’s

68 Georges Rd

A beloved Dominican dining room known for mofongo and guisados. Closes with the dinner hour. 3 Corner keeps the same language of the kitchen, then adds the bar and the late license.

La Cabañita

4 Elizabeth St

Daytime Dominican service and takeout. We share the cuisine; we do not share the clock, the liquor store, or the dance floor.

3 Corner

2 Georges Rd

Restaurant + bar + liquor + grill. Buffet and plated house specials, beer to-go, catering, and a salon for the nights that need a private room.

Events

The salon downstairs.

White linens, navy runners, tufted seating, and a gold 3. The room is built for baptisms, birthdays, and the kind of private dinner that still wants a DJ later.

Catering leaves the same kitchen: picadera boards, pernil, rice, and the juices. Call the house and ask for the event book.
