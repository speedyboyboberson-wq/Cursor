Why people come here

Open later than the falafel shops down the block.

Mamoun’s on Easton closes at 11. Evelyn’s wraps dinner around the same time. King of Gyro keeps the spit going until 2 AM on weeknights and 3 AM on weekends — which is the whole point of a gyro place next to Rutgers.
